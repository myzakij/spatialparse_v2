Supported Expressions

Locate

Absolute Spatial Reference

Direct reference to a named geographic entity
"Find Central Park in New York" "Where is the Eiffel Tower?" "Locate Kazan Kremlin"
  • Place must exist in OpenStreetMap/Nominatim
  • Ambiguous names resolved via LLM context (Portland OR vs ME)
  • Supports Latin and Cyrillic place names
Depends on Nominatim data quality
Relative

Cardinal Direction + Distance

Offset from a place in one of 4 cardinal directions (N/S/E/W)
"5 km south of the Eiffel Tower" "20 km north of Murmansk" "3 km east of Big Ben"
  • Direction must be one of: north, south, east, west
  • Distance must include a numeric value and unit (km, miles, meters)
ADE < 0.01 km (Haversine projection)
Relative

Ordinal Direction + Distance

Offset in one of 4 ordinal directions (NE/NW/SE/SW)
"3 km northeast of Central Park" "7 km southwest of Kazan Kremlin" "10 km southeast of Sydney Opera House"
  • Direction: northeast, northwest, southeast, southwest
  • Bearing fixed at 45/135/225/315 degrees
ADE < 0.01 km
Azimuth

Specific Compass Bearing + Distance

Offset at an exact bearing angle in degrees
"8 km at bearing 120 degrees from Sydney Opera House" "50 km at 210 degrees from Santiago" "4 km at 36 degrees from Jasper"
  • Bearing: 0-360 degrees (0=North, 90=East, 180=South, 270=West)
  • Accepts degree symbol: 120, 120deg, 120 degrees
ADE < 0.01 km
Between

Midpoint Between Two Places

Geographic center point between two locations
"Midpoint between Paris and London" "Halfway between Kremlin and Sheremetyevo" "Center between Colosseum and Pantheon"
  • Both places must be geocodable
  • Uses linear interpolation of coordinates (not great circle)
  • Works at any scale: local (1 km) to international (10000 km)
ADE < 0.05 km
Fraction

Fractional Position Between Two Places

Point at a specific fraction along the line between two locations
"One-quarter of the way from Burwood to Sydney Town Hall" "Three-quarters from Statue of Liberty to Times Square" "Two-thirds of the way from Vladivostok to Khabarovsk"
  • Ratio: 0.0 (at location1) to 1.0 (at location2)
  • Natural language fractions: 'quarter' -> 0.25, 'third' -> 0.33
ADE < 0.3 km (depends on geocoding consistency)
Multiple

Sequential Chain (2 steps)

Two positioning operations applied in sequence
"6 km east of Big Ben, then 4 km south" "15 km NW of Kremlin, then midpoint to Sheremetyevo" "Midpoint of A and B, then 2 km north"
  • Each step references the result of the previous step
  • Supports any combination of Relative, Between, Azimuth, Fraction
ADE < 0.1 km (errors do not accumulate with Haversine)
Multiple

Complex Chain (3+ steps)

Three or more positioning operations in sequence
"From Kremlin 7 km SW, then 4 km azimuth 200, midpoint to Kazan Station, 3 km N, 2 km E" "Start at Golden Gate, 5 km SE, 3 km SW, midpoint to Alcatraz" "From Edinburgh Castle 2 km E, 3 km N, then 4 km at 70 degrees"
  • Maximum tested: 5 steps
  • LLM must correctly parse all step references
  • Parsing accuracy decreases with chain length
ADE < 0.2 km for 3 steps, < 0.5 km for 5 steps
Any

Russian Language Input

Spatial descriptions in Russian, auto-translated to English
"5 km k yugu ot Eiffelevoj bashni" "Seredina mezhdu Kremlyom i Sheremetyevo" "10 km na severo-zapad ot Kazanskogo Kremlya"
  • Cyrillic text auto-detected and translated via LLM
  • Place names converted to standard English (Kazan, Moscow)
  • Directions translated (k severu -> north)
  • Translation cached in SQLite for consistency
Same as English equivalent after translation
Azimuth

Compound Direction Description

Non-standard directional descriptions converted to bearing
"10 degrees west of south -> 190 degrees" "Bearing 045 from location"
  • LLM interprets compound directions and converts to numeric bearing
Depends on LLM interpretation accuracy

Not Supported

NOT SUPPORTED

Temporal-Spatial Expressions

Locations that depend on time or movement
"Where the hurricane will be in 6 hours" "The ship's position tomorrow at noon" "5 km from where I was yesterday"
Reason: System processes static spatial descriptions, not temporal trajectories
NOT SUPPORTED

Self-Referential Positions

Locations relative to the user's current position
"3 km north of here" "The nearest hospital" "5 minutes walk from my location"
Reason: System has no access to user's GPS coordinates
NOT SUPPORTED

Route Planning / Navigation

Optimal paths between locations considering roads
"Fastest route from A to B" "Drive 10 minutes north on Highway 1" "Walking distance to the station"
Reason: System computes geometric positions, not routes. OSRM integration is for visualization only
NOT SUPPORTED

Area Containment / Topology

Spatial relationships like 'inside', 'outside', 'bordering'
"Hotels inside Central Park" "Cities bordering France" "Restaurants within 500m of the river"
Reason: System handles point-based positioning, not polygon intersection queries
NOT SUPPORTED

Vague / Fuzzy Distances

Non-numeric distance descriptions
"A short walk from the station" "Not far from the city center" "A few hours drive south"
Reason: System requires explicit numeric distances. 'nearby' and 'close' are not supported in HGFL pipeline
NOT SUPPORTED

3D / Altitude Positioning

Vertical spatial references
"500 meters above sea level" "The 30th floor of the building" "At the summit of the mountain"
Reason: System operates in 2D (lon/lat). No elevation data used
NOT SUPPORTED

Historical / Non-Existent Places

Places that no longer exist or have changed names
"Constantinople (now Istanbul)" "The Berlin Wall checkpoint" "Stalingrad city center"
Reason: Nominatim indexes current OSM data. Historical names may not resolve. LLM may help with well-known renamings
NOT SUPPORTED

Multiple Result Locations

Queries that should return multiple points
"All Starbucks within 5 km of Times Square" "Three equidistant points between A and B" "Every intersection along Main Street"
Reason: System returns a single result area per query. No POI search capability
NOT SUPPORTED

Indoor / Micro-Scale Positioning

Sub-building level spatial references
"Room 305 in the north wing" "Third aisle of the supermarket" "Gate B12 at the airport"
Reason: Nominatim resolution is ~building level. No indoor mapping
